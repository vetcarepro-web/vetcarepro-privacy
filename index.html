Privacy Policy for VetCarePro

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the VetCarePro mobile application (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by the VetCarePro team (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Your name, email address, and phone number (provided during account registration)
  • Photos of animals, lab reports, and prescriptions uploaded for AI diagnosis and health records
  • Approximate and precise location (used for GPS-based farm mapping and finding nearby veterinarians)
  • Animal health records, weight, breed, and production data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide AI-powered disease diagnosis for livestock and pets
  • Maintain animal health records and vaccination schedules
  • Display nearby veterinarians and suppliers based on your location
  • Deliver telemedicine consultations
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Send reminders for vaccinations, deworming, and appointments

3. Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that have their own Privacy Policy about handling data. Below are the links to the Privacy Policy of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Crashlytics, Analytics, Cloud Messaging)
  • Google ML Kit
  • TensorFlow Lite

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your data is not sold to any third party.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

The Service Provider retains user-provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing vetcarepro.dev@gmail.com. We will respond and delete your data within 30 days.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and maintains. However, no security system is perfect, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the app

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us at:
Email: vetcarepro.dev@gmail.com
