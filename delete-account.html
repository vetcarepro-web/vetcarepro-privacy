Request Account Deletion - VetCarePro

To request deletion of your VetCarePro account and all associated data, please send an email to:

vetcarepro.dev@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your account.

We will process your request within 30 days. The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Your profile data
  • Animal health records associated with your account

Data may be retained in backup systems for up to 30 days after deletion before being permanently purged.

You can also delete your account directly within the VetCarePro app from the Profile screen.
